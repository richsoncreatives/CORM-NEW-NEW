THE LEGACY ENGINE

Build a legacy
of restoration.

Partnership is more than a contribution — it is a shared commitment to helping individuals, couples and communities build stronger foundations. When you partner with CORM, you become part of a movement reaching souls, raising leaders and advancing God's design for relationships.

WHY PARTNER

What we build together lasts longer.

Your partnership helps fuel community outreach, free seminars, public advocacy and the distribution of educational resources to schools, churches and vulnerable communities. It sustains the counseling support we offer and helps us keep practical, biblically grounded teaching accessible to those who need it most.

Choose an ongoing commitment that sustains our work month after month, or support a particular need when you are able. However you give, every partnership begins with a conversation — and every seed makes a difference.

CORM PARTNERSHIP FORMS

Partner with us.

Your partnership helps us reach more lives and strengthen families as we build God's work together. Every gift makes a direct impact — and every partner becomes part of the story.

01 / STEWARD PARTNERS

Be a Steward Partner.

These are faithful men and women who stand with us month after month. Your consistent giving provides the foundation we need to plan, build, and sustain the work God has called us to. You are not just giving; you are stewarding the gospel with us.

“It is required of stewards that they be found faithful.”1 Corinthians 4:2

02 / SUPPORT PARTNERS

Be a Support Partner.

These are friends who give as the Lord leads — occasionally, as needs arise, or as you feel called to sow. There is no ongoing commitment required; you simply give as you are led. Your generous, one-time gifts are powerful answers to prayer and make an immediate impact on our work.

“Because of your partnership in the gospel...”Philippians 1:5

Together, we are reaching souls, raising leaders, and advancing the Kingdom. Thank you for sowing with us.2 Corinthians 9:6–8
