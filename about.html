WHO WE ARE

A calling to build
what lasts.

A trusted community where biblical wisdom and practical counseling come together for healthier lives and relationships. We exist to help people build, repair and strengthen the relationships that shape their destiny.

OUR JOURNEY

Rooted in faith.
Made for real life.

CORM was birthed from a growing global passion for purposeful relationships and God-centered marriages. Recognizing the need for sound relational guidance rooted in Scripture, we bring biblical teachings together with professional counseling methods — meeting people where they are with wisdom that actually works in everyday life.

Through online learning, in-person seminars, conferences, coaching and mentorship, we serve individuals, couples, churches, schools and organizations at every stage of their journey.

Equipping singles & couples for purpose and better marriage.

THE HEART OF CORM

Our vision & mission.

01 / OUR VISION

Love that transforms lives.

Our vision is to become a premier community for those passionate about love, where faith meets practicality to inspire personal and relational development. Through education, coaching and events, we foster a culture of love, forgiveness and understanding grounded in biblical principles and expert advice.

“Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.”EPHESIANS 4:2–3 · NIV

02 / OUR MISSION

Wisdom put into practice.

Our mission is to provide education, counseling and mentorship to individuals, churches, schools and organizations. By focusing first on self-development and blending biblical teaching, seasoned wisdom and proven counseling methods, we help people build healthy, lasting relationships.

“You, however, must teach what is appropriate to sound doctrine.”TITUS 2:1 · NIV

OUR COMMITMENTS

Purpose in every action.
